Selected work

A network foundation two clouds and a datacentre share

Every team solved connectivity its own way, with no highly available path between Google Cloud, on-premise and the second public cloud. I led the build of a redundant interconnect foundation with geo-redundant failover, and a pattern that lets spoke networks attach without bespoke work. It runs in production carrying live workloads, with failover across regions and a measurable latency improvement on the traffic it already moves.

Cloud Interconnect, Cloud Router, VPC, Terraform

Egress policy that runs inside the request, not beside it

Traffic arriving over Private Service Connect cannot be attributed to the consumer behind it, so policy could not be enforced per consumer — and no managed service could do it, which I proved before building anything. I designed and built a control plane that resolves each connection's consumer and decides allow-or-deny in-process inside the proxy, fail-closed, with no network round trip on the decision path. Other teams adopt it through a Terraform provider I wrote.

Envoy, WASM policy filters in Rust, Go, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes, Flux

One path for every new project

New projects, their Terraform workspaces and their infrastructure repositories were created ad hoc, with no consistent model to inherit. I designed the automated path they all take now — creation, labelling, perimeter placement, identity federation, multi-environment support — and the API in front of it. Hundreds of applications have been provisioned on one uniform model.

Terraform, Terraform Cloud, Google Cloud, Open Policy Agent
